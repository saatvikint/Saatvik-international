# Product knowledge

Background only. Nothing here is a claim about Saatvik's products until the client confirms it.

## Makhana (fox nuts)

- Popped seed of *Euryale ferox*, a water plant. India's production is concentrated in Bihar, especially the Mithila region.
- "Mithila Makhana" holds a Geographical Indication tag. Only use it if the client's supply qualifies (Q16).
- The market grades raw makhana by size in **suta**. Marketplace listings commonly advertise 5, 6 and 7 suta, while the client states 4, 5 and 6 (Q8). Larger grades are generally premium.
- Buyers also ask about whiteness, pop rate, moisture, defects and packaging.
- Value-added range: roasted, flavoured, and private-label retail packs.
- Keep packaging moisture-resistant, because moisture hurts crispness. Confirm shelf-life with the client.

## Puffs

- Puffed grain and legume snacks. Client list: millet, jowar, quinoa, chickpea.
- Jowar is sorghum and is itself a millet, so "millet" and "jowar" need clarifying (Q10).
- Puffs are sold plain as an ingredient or snack base, or seasoned as a finished snack. Possible uses include snack mixes, cereals and bars, but confirm what the client supplies.
